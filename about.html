About North Star Bakery

Our Founders

North Star Bakery was founded in 2005 by two bakers, husband and wife Joseph and Sara North. They had a passion for baking and wanted to share their love of baked goods with the world. They started out small, baking in their home kitchen and selling their goods at local farmers markets. As their business grew, they opened their first bakery location in 2010. Today, North Star Bakery has multiple locations and is known for its delicious baked goods and friendly customer service.

Sourcing Philosophy

At North Star Bakery, we believe that the best baked goods start with the best ingredients. That's why we source our ingredients from local farms and suppliers whenever possible. We believe in supporting our local community and economy, and we want to ensure that our customers are getting the freshest and highest quality ingredients in their baked goods. We also believe in using sustainable and ethical practices in our sourcing, and we strive to minimize our environmental impact.

Staff Highlights

Our staff is the heart and soul of North Star Bakery. We are proud to have a team of passionate and dedicated bakers, pastry chefs, and customer service representatives who are committed to providing our customers with the best possible experience. Our staff members are highly trained and skilled in their craft, and they take pride in their work. We believe that happy employees lead to happy customers, and we strive to create a positive and supportive work environment for our team.
